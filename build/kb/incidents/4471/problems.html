The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant writes the day first. Day and month swap: 7 April arrives as 4 July. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type month first, as told, and the form reads it day first. It looks like the parser bug, but the fix belongs in the form. Won’t fix: booking-v3 is retired.
  • shift: no swap. The date moves one day earlier (Tuesday shows as Monday, Lisbon gets Tuesday and Toronto gets Monday). Most likely a time zone or DST conversion. The desk had already read these reports as one-day moves. Open: neither change above touches it.

How the reports were placed

  • Each report is counted once, by id. The ticket export repeats some rows.
  • The tenant decides the cause, not the reporter’s browser. Intake’s tag is a guess and is ignored here (see causes.ts).
  • The desk’s one-day reading (desk-notes.json) marks the shift reports. They stay counted under their tenant’s cause too, as the desk counts them.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. All three problems produce the same subjects (“wrong day”, “date is wrong”), so subject matching cannot keep them apart. Splitting them needs the tenant, the channel and the body.
