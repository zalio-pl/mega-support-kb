The three problems

  • parser: every tenant writes the day first and the parser reads the month first. Day and month swap: 7 April arrives as 4 July.
  • hint: people type month first, as the booking-v3 hint tells them, and the form reads it day first. It looks like the parser bug, but the fix belongs in the form.
  • one-day move: no swap. Tuesday shows as Monday; Lisbon gets Tuesday and Toronto Monday. Most likely a time zone or DST conversion.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. All three problems produce the same subjects, so subject matching cannot keep them apart. Splitting them needs the tenant, the channel and the body.
